ON THIS MAP
All 29 board screens the project keeps or newly designs — Area Overview plus six branches, with PC01’s nine tabs beneath it — 01, 02, 05, 05.1, 05.2, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, PC01 (with PC01.1 state), B1–B4, P01–P04, P06, P08, P12–P15, P17, P18, P24, P25.
DELIBERATELY ABSENT
03 Communities list — removed, membership is derived. 04 Community Network — replaced by PC01. Nine further screens were cut because they already exist elsewhere in Voice 360; they appear here only as DO NOT BUILD · REUSE boxes.
NOT SHOWN AS BOXES
Twelve small states — confirmations, empty, permission and offline — belong to the screens above rather than to the tree. They still need building, alongside the cross-cutting set: loading skeletons, error and offline retry, permission prompts (notifications, contacts, camera for QR, location), pagination and not-verified gating. The board counts eleven existing screens kept; ten are named on it, so one needs confirming.
