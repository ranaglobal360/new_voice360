Handoff
Three rules for using this
Reference roles, never tones
A screen asks for primary, not tone 40 and never a hex. Tones are the factory; roles are the product.
Foregrounds come in pairs
Text on primaryContainer is onPrimaryContainer. If you are choosing a foreground, you have left the system.
Elevation is a surface, not a shadow
Stack the five surfaceContainer steps to show depth. Shadows stay for overlays only — they fail in dark mode.

palette
Why secondary is burnt orange and not a purer orange
At tone 90 the sRGB gamut ceiling flattens every hue between 45° and 70° to almost the same pale peach, so a true orange secondary becomes indistinguishable from errorContainer — measured ΔE 6.5 at hue 57, where anything under 10 cannot be told apart side by side. Error is pinned to M3's canonical 36° and should not move. Secondary therefore sits at 70° (ΔE 12.8 from error) and warning was pushed from Signal Amber's 79° to a truer yellow at 100° to clear it in turn. If you would rather have the purer orange, the alternative is to give every error surface a mandatory error-coloured outline so a pale fill alone never signals error — say the word.
