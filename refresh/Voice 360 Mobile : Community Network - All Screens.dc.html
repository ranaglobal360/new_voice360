Why is it restricted?
Participation follows your area’s direct ancestry. A ward zone reaches upward through its own parents only — never sideways into a neighbouring area, even inside the same district.
swap_vert
If you selected the District instead
A district-level zone would allow participation in every community inside Natore District — subject to stronger verification.
The rule drawn rather than described: the parent chain climbing to the zone, siblings grouped and marked restricted, the reason stated plainly, and the district-level contrast so the policy is understood before anyone requests a change.
