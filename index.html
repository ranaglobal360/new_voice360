Mobile · Concept
Membership is a fact, not a request
Every other civic app asks you to find your community and join it. Voice 360 derives it from your verified address. That one decision removes join, request, pending, approval and leave from the entire product — and it is why one screen can serve all 300 constituencies and every administrative area.
how_to_reg
Derived membership
Address → ward → union → upazila → district → constituency. Verified once.
content_copy
One template, many instances
Names, counts, permissions and content load dynamically. No per-area design.
visibility
Visitor everywhere else
Anything outside your zone opens read-only: view and follow, never post or vote.
Three consequences the design must honour
block
No Join, Request, Pending, Approve or Leave anywhere in the product.
visibility
Permission is stated on screen before the user attempts an action, never discovered by a disabled control.
place
The area is always named explicitly — "Dhaka-10", never "your community".

Do & don't
The rules that keep 300 constituencies, thousands of areas and every business page looking like one product.
check_circleDo
State membership and permission on screen before the user tries anything.
Give every tile a live count — a number is what makes a hub feel current.
Keep one primary action per screen; everything else tinted or outlined.
Reuse the existing module with the area pre-filtered instead of building a parallel one.
Name the area explicitly — "Dhaka-10", never "your community".
Design the empty and restricted state at the same time as the default.
Let containers grow for Bangla rather than truncating.
cancelDon't
No Join, Request, Pending, Approve or Leave anywhere in the product.
No third accent on a screen, and no gradient backgrounds.
No disabled control without an adjacent reason.
No per-area screen, copy or colour variation.
No duplicate messaging, profile or candidate systems.
No hue as body text, no italics, no letter-spacing on paragraphs.
No decorative illustration where real content or a count belongs.
