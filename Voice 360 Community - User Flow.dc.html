→ C10
How to participate here
The only route from visitor to participant is changing the Activity Zone
The rule
Viewing and following are open to everyone. Posting, commenting, reporting, polling and petitioning come only from the Activity Zone and its direct parent chain.
Not Parliamentary
Parliamentary membership is automatic from the verified profile. Community participation must be earned by selecting a zone. Two systems, two join rules.
Blocked on
The Activity Zone is set in Profile U01 / U02, still future phase. Until that ships no citizen can select a zone, so nothing in this map can be used.
