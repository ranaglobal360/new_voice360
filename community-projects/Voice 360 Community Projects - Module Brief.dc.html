Community Projects — Module Brief

15 screens · design complete · August 2026 · source: Community Projects Full Flow PDF

1 · What the module is

Residents propose, fund, and track local improvement projects — tree plantation drives, wells, road and school repairs, health camps — with transparent budgets in taka. Community Projects sits inside the Fundraising area of Voice 360 as journey FR-04 and reuses Fundraising's shared donation components (FR-06 donation sheet, FR-07 payment steps): one payment component serves campaigns, sponsorships, and projects alike.

Transparency is the core theme. A project's expenses, documents, timeline, and funding progress are all public-facing tabs — the Fundraising handover rules apply here too: every capture emits a receipt, organisers never touch money directly, and nothing publishes without verification.

3 · Key user flows

  • Donate: Home → List → Details → Donate — the payment sheet and steps are Fundraising's shared FR-06/FR-07 components.
  • Volunteer: Details → Volunteers tab → join as volunteer.
  • Create: Home → five-step wizard (Basic Info → Media → budget/timeline/review steps shared with campaign create — see open item 8) → live project.
  • Manage: My Projects → Project Dashboard — organiser posts updates, logs expenses, tracks milestones; money itself is never handled by the organiser.

4 · Two progress systems, on purpose

Money and tasks are tracked separately. The progress bar on every project card (CP-01, CP-02) and the goal ring on CP-06 measure funding — ৳ raised against goal. The activity checklist on CP-03 and the milestone phases on CP-10 measure work done. Cards stay money-only; task progress lives in the Timeline tab.

5 · Rules inherited from Fundraising

  • One payment component serves all giving flows (campaigns, sponsorships, projects).
  • Every capture emits a receipt; donations appear in the donor's My Donations and annual statement.
  • Nothing publishes without verification — applies to projects, updates, and expenses.
  • Organisers never touch money directly — funds are ledgered; expenses are a public projection.
  • Route: /fundraising/projects (FR-04).
